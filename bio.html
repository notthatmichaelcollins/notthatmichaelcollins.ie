Biography

Early Life

 

As you've probably already worked out, my name is Michael. I was born in Birmingham, UK on 19th March 1985. I went to Perry Common Junior & Infant School (now called Story Wood School) in Kingstanding, Birmingham, and then Perry Common Comprehensive School, later renamed to College High School (because it was on College Road), now known as North Birmingham Academy. Then I went to Josiah Mason College, which I think is now Pines School. I didn't really have any talents. I still don't really.

 

My first job was at a company called Traveline. I gave out public transport information over the phone for the West Midlands and surrounding areas, including bus and train timetables, and some local prices. I worked there for 8 years before advancements in technology rendered the place almost obsolete. I went to the Jobcentre for a while, then moved to Ireland to be with my girlfriend.

 

Later Life

 

After living with my girlfriend for a while and getting her pregnant, I decided I needed a job. I worked at McDonald's for a while but was let go go with a lot of other people because it wasn't as busy as they expected it to be. I managed to get myself a security license and work in security for a major Facilities Management company. I was there for about 5 years before finding a better paying security job at a major Irish gift retailer. I was there for another few years before getting fired because I wasn't actually very good at being a security guard.

 

So after looking for another job, i got seasonal work at a major grocery store, but that was only for a few weeks, so I didn't have a job for a while after that. I was on jobseeker's payments for a while before joining a Community Employment scheme as a Facilities Management Administrator (the computer guy for the Facilities Managament team), then as a HR Assistant for the HR Manager (computer guy for him). Basically I type up documents when needed, sometimes call companies for services, and help with decorations being put up or taken down. So that's where I am now.
